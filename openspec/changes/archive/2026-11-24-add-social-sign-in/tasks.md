## Test seams (confirmed)

Tests enter only through these seams; anything else is reached through one of them.

1. Pure provider functions (`authorizationParams`, `redirectUri`, `configuredProviders`, `providerNonce`, `appleClientSecret`) with literal vectors.
2. One shared fake issuer through `openid-client`'s custom fetch (keys, discovery and token endpoint), the only provider boundary; `add-native-auth-handoff` reuses its token fixtures.
3. The tracer: a route round trip from the start POST through the fake issuer to the callback.
4. `dispatchIntent` with GoTrue faked at fetch and a real (throwaway) Postgres. GoTrue calls are asserted at the fetch edge (URL, headers, body), never on spies of client factories.
5. `identities.remote.ts` through the `gates.test.ts` pattern.
6. The composed `handle` (hook order, guard, path list).
7. Pure `oauthReturnTarget` and `sameOriginPath`, with `URL.parse` deleted from the global.
8. Browser component tests with mocked remotes.
9. `check:prod` through `harden-auth-codes`' pure `assertAuthConfig(json)`, with literal JSON.
